Her collected essays · 2023

Claiming Our Rights: Feminist Discourses in Bangladesh and South Asia

A collection of essays Dr. Hossain wrote over several decades, following how the women's rights movement in Bangladesh and South Asia developed its arguments over almost fifty years. The essays look at women in policymaking, in markets and in parliament, and at how women organised politically.

The University Press Limited (UPL), Dhaka · ISBN 9789845064019

Author · 1988

The Company Weavers of Bengal: The East India Company and the Organization of Textile Production in Bengal, 1750-1813

Hameeda Hossain

A revised version of Dr. Hossain's Oxford DPhil thesis (1982). It uses newly found eighteenth-century Bengali records of the East India Company to argue that the Company's push for low wages and strict control over weavers helped bring down the Bengal weaving industry, and it describes the weavers' lives and working conditions.

Oxford University Press, Delhi (Oxford University South Asian Studies Series), 1988; Bangladesh edition: The University Press Limited, Dhaka, 2010 · ISBN 0195620437 (OUP 1988; ISBN-13 9780195620436); 9789848815069 (UPL 2010)

Of the Nation Born: The Bangladesh Papers

Hameeda Hossain and Amena Mohsin (eds); introduction by Meghna Guhathakurta

The Bangladesh volume of Zubaan's IDRC-supported series on sexual violence and impunity in South Asia. It brings together essays, interviews and case studies on sexual violence from the 1971 war onward, covering law, nationalism, memory, minorities and the feminist campaigns for recognition and accountability.

Zubaan, New Delhi (Zubaan Series on Sexual Violence and Impunity in South Asia); distributed by the University of Chicago Press · ISBN 9789384757793 (ISBN-10 9384757799)

Amena Mohsin and Hameeda Hossain (eds)

The Bengali translation and expanded edition of Of the Nation Born. Alongside the translated essays it adds interviews, case studies, and extracts from poems, novels and stories about sexual violence in 1971 and the impunity that followed.

Co-author · 2010

Freedom from Fear, Freedom from Want? Re-thinking Security in Bangladesh

Hameeda Hossain, Meghna Guhathakurta and Malini Sur

A short study of how state formation and political governance in Bangladesh create fear and insecurity for women by putting male experience at the centre. It treats both the state and globalising markets as sources of insecurity, especially for women from religious minorities, and uses the ideas of 'freedom from fear' and 'freedom from want' as its frame.

Rupa & Co., New Delhi, for WISCOMP (Women in Security, Conflict Management and Peace); series: Revisioning and EnGendering Security: Gender and Non-Traditional Aspects of Security in South Asia · ISBN 9788129115591

Contributor · chapter 5 · 2014

Human Rights in Bangladesh: Past, Present and Future

Imtiaz Ahmed (ed.); 12 contributors including Hameeda Hossain

An edited volume of twelve essays and an introduction on human rights in Bangladesh, arranged in past, present and future sections. Dr. Hossain's Chapter 5, 'Human Rights in the Nation State' (in Part II, the present), argues that narrow readings of democracy, secularism and nationalism have drawn the state away from its founding ideals, and that powerful interests limit access to justice.

The University Press Limited (UPL), Dhaka · ISBN 9789845061520 (first edition); UPL also lists a second edition, ISBN 9789845064675

Rising from the Ashes: Women's Narratives of 1971

Editorial board: Shaheen Akhtar, Suraiya Begum, Meghna Guhathakurta, Hameeda Hossain, Sultana Kamal; translated by Niaz Zaman; introduction by Mofidul Hoque; foreword by Hameeda Hossain

The English translation of ASK's 2001 Bengali collection of oral histories, Narir Ekattor. It gathers the testimonies of 22 women who lived through killings, rape, eviction and displacement in 1971, and their later struggles for survival and justice.

Ain o Salish Kendra (ASK) and The University Press Limited (UPL), Dhaka (Road to Bangladesh series) · ISBN 9789845060448

Gendered Lives, Livelihood and Transformation: The Bangladesh Context

Meghna Guhathakurta and Ayesha Banu (eds)

An anthology of fieldwork-based studies of women's lives and work in Bangladesh as it moved from a subsistence economy to a capitalist one. Its subjects include indigenous women in jhum cultivation, Dalit women, female-headed rural households and elderly women in city slums.

The University Press Limited (UPL), Dhaka · ISBN 9789845062466

Human Rights in Bangladesh (Ain o Salish Kendra annual report series)

Ain o Salish Kendra; edited by Hameeda Hossain for several years

ASK's yearly review of the human rights situation in Bangladesh, covering law enforcement, violence against women, minorities, freedom of expression and access to justice. Dr. Hossain edited many volumes of the series in the 2000s.
