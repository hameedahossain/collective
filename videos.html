Video & audio

Recorded interviews, oral histories and television conversations. Each opens where it was first published.

  • 2021 · Desh TV · Interviewed by Asaduzzaman Noor · November 21, 2021 বেলা অবেলা সারাবেলা (Bela Obela Sarabela (Morning, Noon and Night)) ↗ An episode of the Desh TV conversation programme Bela Obela Sarabela in which Hameeda Hossain talks with actor and politician Asaduzzaman Noor. Video
  • 2012 · 1947 Partition Archives · Interviewed by Farhana Afroz · Date February 2012 Life in Hyderabad, Sindh During 1947 ↗ A short 1947 Partition Archive video in which she recalls her childhood in Sindh: her father, a judge who educated his daughters, a school emptied of Hindu, Sikh and Christian pupils after Partition, and helping at her grandmother's refugee clinic. Video
  • ❦ 2011 · Tufts University · Interviewed by Iftekhar Iqbal · January 20, 2011 Bengali Intellectuals Oral History Project: Interview with Hameeda Hossain ↗ A long oral history covering her childhood in Sindh, Wellesley, publishing work, editing Forum in 1960s Dhaka, the events of March 1971, return to Bangladesh in 1972, Oxford research on Bengal weavers, exile after 1975, Karika and founding ASK. Audio
  • 2011 · Secularism Is A Women's Issue (SIAWI) · Conducted by Marieme Helie Lucas · April 2011 Women Speak on Secularism ↗ A video interview from Dhaka recorded by Marieme Helie Lucas for the Secularism Is A Women's Issue series, part of a set of conversations with feminists on secularism, fundamentalisms and women's rights. Video
