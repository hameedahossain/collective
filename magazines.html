Magazines

Magazine pieces by and about her, and the publications she helped build, among them Forum, the weekly she co-founded with Rehman Sobhan and Kamal Hossain.

  • 2012 Canvas — "Cases of Violence Against Women Somehow Get Deactivated" A Women's Day interview in which she discusses patriarchy and violence against women, sexual harassment, fatwa cases, weak policing and courts that let cases lapse, family-based politics that does not empower women, and the organised women's movement. Magazine
  • 1986 Mainstream Her "Dhaka Letter" for an Indian weekly describes the May 1986 parliamentary election under General Ershad, with organised violence, booth capture and manipulated results as the Jatiyo Party sought a two-thirds majority to indemnify martial law. Magazine
